You are an expert accessibility-first web designer and full-stack engineer specializing in nonprofit, ethical AI, and disability-inclusive websites.

Build a modern, scalable, and fully accessible website for a nonprofit whose mission is to use AI for human good. The organization supports multiple current and future initiatives. One flagship initiative is an already-built bone-conduction hearing assist mobile app for people with conductive hearing loss, but the website must not be single-app focused.

GOALS
- Clearly communicate an “AI for Human Good” mission
- Support multiple initiatives via reusable, modular page templates
- Build trust with beneficiaries, donors, partners, and grantmakers
- Encourage app adoption, education, advocacy, and donations
- Meet or exceed WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility standards

DESIGN DIRECTION (2025 WEB)
- Minimalist, warm, and human-centered
- Generous white space and clear hierarchy
- Subtle glassmorphism for cards (soft blur, low opacity)
- Soft shadows (no harsh contrast)
- Color palette:
  - Primary: calm blues and greens
  - Accent: teal or amber
- Typography:
  - Sans-serif (Inter, Source Sans 3, or system equivalent)
  - Base font size ≥18px
  - Strong line height and spacing
- Fully responsive, mobile-first
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion

ACCESSIBILITY (NON-NEGOTIABLE)
- WCAG 2.2 AA (AAA where feasible)
- Keyboard-only navigation
- Screen reader support (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver)
- High contrast and dark mode support
- Text resizing up to 200%
- No audio-only communication
- Captions and transcripts for all video
- Semantic HTML, proper ARIA roles
- Icons must always include text labels

REQUIRED PAGES
1. Home
   - Clear AI-for-good mission above the fold
   - Featured initiatives in a scalable card layout
   - CTAs: Explore Our Work, Get Involved, Support the Mission

2. About
   - Mission, vision, values
   - Ethical AI principles
   - Accessibility and inclusion commitment
   - Governance and transparency

3. Our Initiatives (Hub)
   - Modular, reusable initiative cards
   - Each initiative includes:
     - Problem
     - Who it helps
     - AI solution overview
     - Impact and status

4. Initiative Detail Template (Reusable)
   - Human-centered explanation
   - Visual diagrams (no sound reliance)
   - Ethics, privacy, and data practices
   - How to participate or benefit

5. Hearing Accessibility Initiative
   - Explains bone-conduction hearing assist app
   - Plain-language explanation
   - Supported devices
   - App download links
   - Privacy and safety assurances

6. Accessibility & Ethics
   - Accessibility statement
   - Ethical AI framework
   - Bias mitigation
   - Community-first design principles

7. Impact & Stories
   - Text-first testimonials
   - Quantitative impact metrics
   - Captioned video optional

8. Get Involved
   - Volunteers
   - Partnerships
   - Research and advocacy

9. Donate / Support
   - Nonprofit status
   - Donation transparency
   - One-time and recurring donations
   - Grant support information

10. Resources
    - AI ethics education
    - Accessibility guides
    - Downloadable accessible PDFs

11. Contact
    - Fully accessible contact form
    - Multiple contact options

TECHNICAL CONSTRAINTS
- Use modern web stack
- Prefer Next.js, Astro, or Nuxt
- Tailwind CSS or CSS variables for styling
- CMS-friendly structure for initiatives
- No autoplay media
- No inaccessible CAPTCHA

CONTENT TONE
- Plain language (≈8th-grade reading level)
- Empowering, respectful, non-clinical
- Avoid unnecessary AI jargon
- Focus on dignity, independence, and trust

OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS
Provide:
1. Site map and information architecture
2. Modular component structure
3. Page-by-page UX breakdown
4. Accessibility considerations per page
5. Example copy for key sections
6. Technical implementation notes
7. Risks and mitigation strategies

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
The website should feel human, ethical, calm, and future-ready, clearly communicating that AI can be both powerful and compassionate.
